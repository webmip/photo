```

luminous skin. Rich, pure red backdrop with no texture or distraction. Solid color

skin tones. Clean, minimalist styling focusing attention on face and lighting. 
Professional, composed expression with quiet confidence.

Composition: Vertical portrait format (9:16 ratio, 1080x1920), centered subject with 
headroom, dramatic upward perspective. Cinematic framing with bold color blocking.

Technical: Professional cinematic photography, high contrast lighting, rich color 
saturation, sharp facial details, smooth skin rendering with natural texture, 
editorial quality finish.

Ultra high-resolution, photorealistic, cinematic color grading, dramatic lighting 
setup, sculptural quality, intense visual impact, 1080x1920 vertical format.
```

## 💡 Tips and Variations

- **Face Consistency:** Upload the reference image to Perplexity and explicitly mention maintaining exact facial features for identity preservation.
- **Background Colors:** Try "deep navy blue", "pure black", or "forest green" for different dramatic color contrasts.
- **Lighting Variations:** Experiment with "Rembrandt lighting", "split lighting", or "butterfly lighting" for different dramatic effects.
- **Camera Angles:** Adjust to "eye level with side tilt", "extreme low angle", or "slight high angle" for varied perspectives.
- **Wardrobe Colors:** Try "white shirt for inverse contrast", "burgundy to match background", or "gray for subtle tones".
- **Expression Variations:** Modify to "intense gaze", "serene expression", or "contemplative look" for different moods.

## 🎬 Cinematic Photography Techniques

- **Low Angle Power:** Upward-facing camera creates psychological dominance and heroic presence
- **Stark Contrast:** High contrast lighting creates sculptural, three-dimensional quality
- **Color Blocking:** Solid crimson background creates bold graphic impact
- **Luminous Skin:** Contrast between dark clothing and illuminated skin draws eye to face
- **Sculptural Lighting:** Directional light emphasizes bone structure and facial geometry

## 📝 Additional Notes

**Reference Image Usage:** This prompt continues the series using the same reference image, demonstrating GPT-5's ability to maintain facial consistency across different dramatic lighting scenarios and formats.

**Vertical Format Specifications:**
- **Resolution:** 1080x1920 pixels
- **Aspect Ratio:** 9:16 (vertical)
- **Use Cases:** Social media stories, mobile content, vertical displays
- **Advantages:** Mobile-optimized, portrait-focused, dramatic height

**Low Angle Psychology:** The upward-facing camera angle creates several effects:
- **Power Dynamic:** Subject appears more dominant and authoritative
- **Sculptural Quality:** Emphasizes jawline, neck, and facial structure
- **Heroic Framing:** Common in cinematic hero shots and fashion editorials
- **Architectural Feel:** Creates strong vertical lines and geometric composition

**Color Psychology - Crimson Red:**
- **Passion & Power:** Bold, commanding, confident
- **Visual Impact:** High saturation creates immediate attention
- **Contrast Maximizer:** Perfect backdrop for luminous skin tones
- **Cinematic Drama:** Classic color for dramatic portraits

**Lighting Setup:**
- Stark, high-contrast lighting (likely single key light)
- Strong directional source creating defined shadows
- Minimal fill light to maintain drama
- Possible rim light for edge definition

**Best Use Cases:**
- Social media profile portraits
- Instagram Stories and Reels
- TikTok content
- Mobile app interfaces
- Vertical display advertising
- Fashion editorial verticals
- Music artist promotional content
- Personal branding photography
- Magazine vertical spreads

**Technical Considerations:**
- Vertical format requires careful composition to avoid empty space
- Low angle must be balanced to avoid unflattering distortion
- Red background requires proper color calibration to avoid oversaturation
- High contrast needs careful exposure to preserve detail in shadows
- Skin luminosity should appear natural, not artificially brightened
