- **Neon Color Schemes:** Try "cyan and magenta", "green and orange", or "red and blue" for different cyberpunk color palettes.

## 🌟 Neon Photography Techniques

- **Color Temperature Contrast:** Cool neon blues/purples against warm skin tones creates visual tension
- **Rim Lighting:** Neon tubes create natural rim lighting that separates subject from background
- **Reflective Surfaces:** Chrome and glossy materials multiply the neon effect through reflections
- **Atmospheric Haze:** Light diffusion through haze creates depth and cinematic quality
- **Color Harmony:** Purple-pink-blue palette is complementary and visually striking
