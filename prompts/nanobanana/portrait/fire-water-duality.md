## 🎨 Prompt

```
Epic cinematic hyper-realistic portrait of two identical men standing back to back 
in perfect symmetry. Use exact facial features from reference photo, preserve all face 
proportions, skin tone, hairstyle, and expression exactly. Both figures must be identical 
with ultra-consistent identity. Maintain photorealism with extreme detail.

Left Figure - Fire Side: Man engulfed in roaring flames, glowing embers swirling around 
scorched leather jacket. Illuminated by fiery orange light, warm tones dominating. 
Fire licking across clothing and surrounding air. Intense heat glow on skin. Dramatic 
orange and red color palette.

Right Figure - Water Side: Man drenched in cascading water, droplets flowing across face 
and soaked jacket. Glowing with icy blue tones, cool color palette. Water streaming down 
body, splashing effects. Wet fabric clinging to form. Cold blue illumination on skin.

Center Collision: At the center where their backs meet, fire and water violently collide, 
creating dramatic explosion of steam, sparks, and splashing droplets. Elemental clash 
with visible energy. Mist and vapor rising from collision point. Dynamic interaction 
between opposing elements.

Expression: Both look intense and unyielding, symbolizing eternal clash of heat and cold. 
Strong, determined expressions. Powerful presence and commanding stance.

Technical: Ultra-detailed skin textures showing pores and natural imperfections. Realistic 
elemental effects with accurate fire behavior and water physics. Cinematic 8K portrait 
photography with dramatic lighting contrast. Sharp focus on faces. Professional color 
grading separating warm and cool sides.

Composition: Perfect symmetry, back-to-back positioning, centered collision point. 
Balanced composition with equal visual weight on both sides. Epic scale and presence.

Photorealistic, hyperdetailed, 8K ultra resolution, cinematic lighting, dramatic contrast, 
realistic fire and water effects, professional photography, extreme detail on skin and 
elements, epic cinematic quality.
```
