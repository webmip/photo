## 🎨 Prompt

```
Epic cinematic hyper-realistic portrait of two identical men standing back to back 
in perfect symmetry. Use exact facial features from reference photo, preserve all face 
proportions, skin tone, hairstyle, and expression exactly. Both figures must be identical 
with ultra-consistent identity. Maintain photorealism with extreme detail.

Left Figure - Fire Side: Man engulfed in roaring flames, glowing embers swirling around 
scorched leather jacket. Illuminated by fiery orange light, warm tones dominating. 
Fire licking across clothing and surrounding air. Intense heat glow on skin. Dramatic 
orange and red color palette.

Right Figure - Water Side: Man drenched in cascading water, droplets flowing across face 
and soaked jacket. Glowing with icy blue tones, cool color palette. Water streaming down 
body, splashing effects. Wet fabric clinging to form. Cold blue illumination on skin.

Center Collision: At the center where their backs meet, fire and water violently collide, 
creating dramatic explosion of steam, sparks, and splashing droplets. Elemental clash 
with visible energy. Mist and vapor rising from collision point. Dynamic interaction 
between opposing elements.

Expression: Both look intense and unyielding, symbolizing eternal clash of heat and cold. 
Strong, determined expressions. Powerful presence and commanding stance.

Technical: Ultra-detailed skin textures showing pores and natural imperfections. Realistic 
elemental effects with accurate fire behavior and water physics. Cinematic 8K portrait 
photography with dramatic lighting contrast. Sharp focus on faces. Professional color 
grading separating warm and cool sides.

Composition: Perfect symmetry, back-to-back positioning, centered collision point. 
Balanced composition with equal visual weight on both sides. Epic scale and presence.

Photorealistic, hyperdetailed, 8K ultra resolution, cinematic lighting, dramatic contrast, 
realistic fire and water effects, professional photography, extreme detail on skin and 
elements, epic cinematic quality.
```

## 💡 Tips and Variations

- **Face Consistency:** The reference image instruction ensures both figures maintain identical facial features. NanoBanana excels at creating perfect symmetry with consistent identity.
- **Element Variations:** Try "ice and lava", "lightning and earth", or "light and shadow" for different elemental contrasts.
- **Color Schemes:** Experiment with "blue fire vs red water" for inverse color psychology, or "golden fire vs silver water" for metallic tones.
- **Clothing Styles:** Change to "formal suits", "tactical gear", or "casual wear" for different aesthetic contexts.
- **Background:** Add "cosmic background", "abstract void", or "urban environment" for additional context.
- **Collision Effects:** Modify to "gentle mist meeting" or "explosive energy burst" for different intensity levels.

## 🔥💧 Elemental Photography Techniques

- **Color Temperature Contrast:** Warm orange/red (fire) vs cool blue (water) creates maximum visual impact
- **Symmetrical Composition:** Perfect mirror positioning emphasizes duality concept
- **Elemental Physics:** Realistic fire behavior (upward movement, embers) and water physics (downward flow, droplets)
- **Collision Dynamics:** Steam and vapor at meeting point shows realistic elemental interaction
- **Lighting Separation:** Each side lit by its respective element creates natural color zones

## 📝 Additional Notes

**Reference Image Usage:** This prompt demonstrates NanoBanana's advanced capability to create identical duplicates of the same person while maintaining perfect facial consistency across both figures, even with dramatically different lighting and effects.

**Technical Complexity:** This is an expert-level prompt combining:
- Perfect symmetrical duplication
- Realistic fire simulation (embers, glow, heat distortion)
- Realistic water physics (droplets, flow, splash)
- Elemental collision effects (steam, sparks, mist)
- Dual lighting scenarios (warm vs cool)
- Extreme detail preservation

**Elemental Realism:**
- **Fire:** Should show natural flame behavior with upward movement, ember particles, heat glow, and orange/red color spectrum
- **Water:** Should display realistic fluid dynamics with droplet formation, streaming flow, splash effects, and blue/cyan tones
- **Collision:** Steam formation where elements meet is physically accurate (water evaporating from heat)

**Symmetry Importance:** Perfect back-to-back positioning creates:
- Visual balance and harmony
- Strong conceptual impact
- Professional composition
- Emphasis on duality theme

**Lighting Challenges:**
- Fire side: Warm, dynamic, flickering light
- Water side: Cool, reflective, fluid light
- Center: Mixed lighting with steam diffusion
- Faces: Must remain clearly visible despite dramatic effects
