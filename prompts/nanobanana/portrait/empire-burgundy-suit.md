```

a red wall. Use exact facial features from reference photo, preserve all face proportions, 
skin tone, hairstyle, and expression exactly. Ultra-consistent identity across generations.

Outfit: Burgundy three-piece suit (jacket, vest, trousers), crisp white dress shirt, 
red silk tie. Red sneakers with white soles adding casual urban touch to formal attire. 
Watch on left wrist. Impeccable tailoring and fit.

Appearance: Neatly styled brown hair, light beard, well-groomed, confident demeanor.

Pose: Standing confidently, hands clasped at waist level, upright posture, commanding 
presence. Professional yet approachable stance.

Background: Bold red wall with large white letters spelling "EMPIRE" in clean, modern 
typography. Subtle light-colored star logo in bottom right corner. Dark wood floor 
creating grounded base.

Lighting: Even, professional lighting with soft shadows. Clean editorial lighting that 
highlights suit texture and fabric details. Balanced exposure across subject and background.

Composition: Full-length portrait, centered subject, vertical format. Fashion editorial 
framing showing complete outfit from head to toe. Strong color coordination between 
burgundy suit, red wall, and red sneakers.

Technical: Professional fashion photography, sharp focus on face and suit details, 
rich burgundy tones, crisp white contrast, textured fabric rendering, polished editorial 
finish.

Ultra high-resolution 8K, hyperdetailed textures on suit fabric, photorealistic, 
professional fashion editorial, GQ magazine style, extreme detail on tailoring and materials.
```
