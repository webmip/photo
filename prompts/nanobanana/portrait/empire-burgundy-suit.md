burgundy suit, red wall, and red sneakers.

Technical: Professional fashion photography, sharp focus on face and suit details, 
rich burgundy tones, crisp white contrast, textured fabric rendering, polished editorial

Ultra high-resolution 8K, hyperdetailed textures on suit fabric, photorealistic, 
professional fashion editorial, GQ magazine style, extreme detail on tailoring and materials.

## 💡 Tips and Variations

- **Face Consistency:** The reference image instruction ensures facial features remain identical. NanoBanana excels at maintaining professional, confident expressions.
- **Suit Colors:** Try "navy blue suit", "charcoal gray suit", or "black suit" for different corporate aesthetics while keeping the red wall.
- **Wall Text:** Experiment with different words like "SUCCESS", "POWER", "LEGACY" for varied messaging.
- **Sneaker Variations:** Change to "white sneakers", "black dress shoes", or "brown leather shoes" for different style statements.
- **Background Colors:** Try "white wall with black text", "navy wall with gold text", or "gray wall with white text" for alternative color schemes.
- **Accessories:** Add "sunglasses", "pocket square", or "briefcase" for additional styling elements.

## 👔 Fashion Editorial Techniques

- **High-Low Mix:** The combination of formal three-piece suit with casual red sneakers creates modern, approachable luxury aesthetic
- **Color Coordination:** Burgundy suit + red wall + red sneakers creates cohesive, bold color story
- **Typography Integration:** "EMPIRE" text adds branding element and aspirational messaging
- **Power Posing:** Hands clasped at waist conveys confidence, control, and executive presence
- **Texture Contrast:** Smooth suit fabric vs. textured wall vs. leather sneakers adds visual interest
