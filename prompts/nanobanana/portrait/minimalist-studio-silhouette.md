## 💡 Tips and Variations

- **Face Consistency:** The reference image instruction ensures 100% facial preservation. NanoBanana excels at maintaining reserved, controlled expressions when explicitly instructed.
- **Lighting Variations:** Try "top-down strip light" or "bottom-up dramatic light" for different silhouette effects while keeping the minimalist aesthetic.
- **Background Colors:** Experiment with "pure white background" or "dark gray background" for different contrast levels.
- **Clothing Variations:** Try "white shirt and black pants" for inverse contrast, or "all gray outfit" for softer tones.
- **Pose Adjustments:** Modify to "hands in pockets" or "one hand on wall" for subtle pose variations.
- **Lighting Intensity:** Adjust to "soft side light" for gentler shadows or "hard strip light" for more dramatic contrast.

## 🎨 Minimalist Photography Techniques

- **Negative Space:** The black background creates powerful negative space, emphasizing the subject's silhouette
- **Chiaroscuro:** Strong light-dark contrast creates dramatic, sculptural quality
- **Monochromatic Palette:** All-black clothing and background focus attention on form and lighting
- **Edge Lighting:** Side strip light defines body contours and creates separation from background
- **Reserved Expression:** Controlled, mysterious demeanor adds sophistication and intrigue
