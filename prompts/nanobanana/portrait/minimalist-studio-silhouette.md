# Minimalist Studio Silhouette Portrait

## 📝 General Information

- **Model:** NanoBanana
- **Category:** Portrait
- **Style:** Minimalist Studio Photography
- **Difficulty:** Intermediate
- **Reference Image:** [Pixabay Reference](https://pixabay.com/es/photos/tall-man-entrenador-joven-body-1203884/)

## 🎨 Prompt

```
A minimalist studio portrait in hyper-realistic style. Person leaning against black wall 
in minimalist studio with black walls. Use exact facial features from reference photo, 
preserve all face proportions, skin tone, hairstyle, and expression exactly. Maintain 
100% facial consistency and reserved expression. Ultra-consistent identity across generations.

Outfit: Black coat over dark turtleneck sweater and fitted pants. All black clothing 
creating monochromatic aesthetic.

Pose: Arms crossed, body slightly turned to the side. Confident yet relaxed stance 
leaning against wall.

Expression: Intense and serene gaze, lips firmly closed, reserved demeanor. Calm, 
introspective, mysterious presence.

Lighting: Single strip of side lighting highlighting the contour of the figure, creating 
mysterious silhouette effect. Dramatic rim light separating subject from black background. 
Strong contrast between light and shadow, emphasizing body outline and facial features.

Composition: Studio portrait, centered subject against pure black background. Minimalist 
aesthetic with focus on silhouette and form. Clean, simple, elegant framing.

Technical: Professional studio photography, high contrast black and white aesthetic, 
sharp focus on face and silhouette edges, soft gradient lighting on body, dramatic 
chiaroscuro effect.

Ultra high-resolution 8K, hyperdetailed textures, photorealistic, professional fashion 
photography, editorial quality, extreme detail on fabric and skin texture.
```
