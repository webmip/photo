# Vintage Car Night Portrait

## 🎨 Prompt

```
A cinematic hyper-realistic portrait of a person sitting inside a vintage red car at night. 
Use exact facial features from reference photo, preserve all face proportions, skin tone, 
hairstyle, and expression exactly. Ultra-consistent identity across generations.

Scene: Inside vintage red car with warm brown leather interior. Rainy night with raindrops 
clearly visible on side window. Soft reflections of neon lights dancing on wet glass.

Lighting: Moody and cinematic. Neon lighting in red and green tones illuminates the scene, 
casts cinematic highlights and subtle reflections across face and glasses. Soft yet 
directional lighting creating perfect night-time atmosphere.

Composition: Close-up profile angle from outside the car. Car's window frame included in shot. 
Subject sitting slightly leaned back, gazing thoughtfully outside with calm, introspective 
expression. Background softly blurred with glowing city lights, emphasizing depth and mood.

Technical: Shot with 35mm f/1.4 lens for creamy depth of field. Realistic reflections and 
water droplets on glass. Neon reflections accurately matching reference. Natural skin texture 
with sharp details on glasses and face. Cinematic contrast, slightly desaturated colors for 
dreamy look.

Ultra high-resolution 8K, hyperdetailed textures, realistic grain for cinematic filmic look, 
professional photography, photorealistic, extreme detail.
```

## 💡 Tips and Variations

- **Face Consistency:** Always include the reference image instruction to maintain identity across generations. NanoBanana excels at preserving facial features when explicitly instructed.
- **Neon Colors:** Experiment with different neon color combinations (blue/pink, purple/orange) for varied moods while keeping the same composition.
- **Weather Variations:** Try "foggy night" or "clear night with stars" instead of rain for different atmospheres.
- **Car Interior:** Change to "black leather interior" or "beige fabric seats" for different vintage aesthetics.
- **Time of Day:** Adapt to "golden hour sunset" or "blue hour twilight" for warmer/cooler tones.
- **Camera Angle:** Try "front-facing through windshield" or "side profile from passenger seat" for composition variety.

## 🎬 Cinematic Techniques

- **Depth of Field:** The 35mm f/1.4 specification creates beautiful bokeh in the background city lights
- **Color Grading:** Slightly desaturated colors give a cinematic film look reminiscent of neo-noir photography
- **Reflections:** The layered reflections (neon on glass, glass on face) add visual complexity
- **Rain Elements:** Water droplets add texture and authenticity to the night scene
