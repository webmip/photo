# Ink Sketch Drawing Portrait

## 📝 General Information

- **Model:** NanoBanana
- **Category:** Artistic
- **Style:** Photo-style Ink Sketch / Line Drawing
- **Difficulty:** Advanced
- **Reference Image:** [Pixabay Reference](https://pixabay.com/es/photos/tall-man-entrenador-joven-body-1203884/)

## 🎨 Prompt

```
Create a photo-style line drawing / ink sketch of a face identical to the uploaded 
reference image. Keep every facial feature, proportion, and expression exactly the same. 
Use exact facial features from reference photo, preserve all face proportions, skin tone 
representation through line work, hairstyle, and expression exactly. Ultra-consistent 
identity in sketch form.

Style: Blue and white ink tones with intricate, fine line detailing. Delicate cross-hatching 
and stippling techniques. Clean, precise linework showing artistic skill. Hand-drawn aesthetic 
with visible ink texture and pen strokes.

Background: Notebook-page style background with subtle ruled lines or grid pattern. White 
or off-white paper texture. Realistic paper grain and slight imperfections.

Composition: Right hand holding a pen near the sketch, as if the artist is still working. 
Hand also holding an eraser. Natural hand position showing active drawing process. Pen tip 
close to the drawing suggesting work in progress.

Details: Intricate fine line detailing capturing facial features through linework. Blue ink 
for main sketch lines, white highlights for paper texture. Realistic pen and eraser objects. 
Authentic hand anatomy and natural grip on drawing tools.

Technical: Photorealistic rendering of a hand-drawn sketch. Sharp focus on ink lines and 
facial details. Realistic paper texture and ink behavior. Natural lighting showing drawing 
on flat surface. Meta-realistic approach - photorealistic image of an artistic sketch.

Ultra high-resolution 8K, hyperdetailed line work, photorealistic hand and tools, authentic 
ink sketch aesthetic, professional illustration quality, extreme detail on pen strokes and 
facial line work.
```
